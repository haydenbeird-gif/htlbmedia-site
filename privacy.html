Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how HTLB MEDIA LLC ("HTLB MEDIA," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses and protects information when you visit www.htlbmedia.com, contact us, or use any application or service we operate, including applications that connect to Meta platforms such as Facebook and Instagram.

Information we collect

  • Information you provide: your name, phone number, email address and any details you share when you contact us or become a client.
  • Platform information: when you authorize one of our applications to connect to a social media account, we receive only the information and permissions you approve, such as your page or account name, profile ID, and content or insights needed to provide our services.
  • Technical information: basic data such as browser type, device type and pages visited, which may be recorded by our hosting provider in standard server logs.

How we use information

  • To provide, manage and improve our content and social media management services.
  • To publish, schedule or analyze content on accounts you have authorized us to manage.
  • To respond to your questions and communicate with you about our services.
  • To comply with legal obligations and protect our rights.

Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with service providers who help us operate our business (such as hosting and scheduling tools), with the platforms you have authorized us to act on, or when required by law.

Data from Meta platforms

Data received through Meta platforms is used only to provide the services you requested and in accordance with Meta's Platform Terms. We do not use this data for advertising profiles, and we do not transfer it to data brokers.

Data retention and deletion

We keep personal information only as long as needed to provide our services or meet legal requirements. You may request that we delete your information, including any data received from Meta platforms, at any time by calling us at 360-977-0256 or writing to us at the address below. We will confirm and complete deletion requests within 30 days. You can also remove our application's access at any time from your Facebook or Instagram settings.

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information from unauthorized access.

Children's privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
